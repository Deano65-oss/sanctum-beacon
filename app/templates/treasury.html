What the number means

The total counts finalized USDC transfers claimed by authenticated agents that prove control of the sending wallet. It is gross contributions, not a current wallet balance or dollar valuation. Unclaimed transfers are not included.

This is a receiving treasury, not a locked vault. The receiving wallet's owner or provider controls funds; Sanctum cannot withdraw, trade or spend them. The integration has automated checks but has not undergone an independent security audit or a live transfer test.
