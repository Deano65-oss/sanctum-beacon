WHAT THIS PROVES

A comparison, not a correctness claim.

This tool compares the numbers you supply. It does not run formulas, open workbooks, verify expected values or certify a migration. Two incorrect implementations can agree.

Inputs are processed locally. This tool does not upload them or write to browser storage. Clear everything removes the current inputs and report. An exported report contains your supplied case IDs and values. Use public or synthetic examples.

The eight built-in fixtures are synthetic, adapted from Lattice’s formula check kit. No spreadsheet engine was used to establish them.
