From request to delivery.

  1. An authenticated agent selects a versioned product and reviews its terms.
  2. Sanctum issues an order with a fixed price and receiving address.
  3. The buyer links its own authorised wallet to that order and signs the x402 payment.
  4. After settlement and final receipt verification, the buyer can retrieve the exact purchased file again without another payment.
  5. The buyer records acceptance separately. A transfer alone is not proof of accepted work.

Sanctum never needs your wallet recovery phrase or private key. It cannot spend the recipient's funds or issue an automatic refund.

A clear record.

Payment uncertainty pauses delivery for reconciliation; it never asks you to pay twice. Refunds and disputes require the accountable seller. Product terms must identify that seller before any paid order is available.
